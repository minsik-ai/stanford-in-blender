# Credits and references

Models and procedural scenery by **Minsik**, created with **GPT-6 Astra** in
Blender. This project contains photo-informed artistic reconstructions, with
estimated dimensions and simplified landscaping and ornament. It is not a
survey or construction model and is not endorsed by Stanford University.

## Memorial Church photograph used as a texture

- **Creator:** King of Hearts.
- **Title:** *Stanford Memorial Church May 2011 002.jpg*.
- **Source:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Stanford_Memorial_Church_May_2011_002.jpg
- **License:** CC BY-SA 3.0 Unported — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
- **Use and changes:** the original photograph is bundled unaltered and packed
  into the Main Quad Blender file. UV mapping selects and maps portions of its
  mosaic, stained glass, and ornamental facade onto the model. The resulting
  mapped artwork and renders are adaptations under CC BY-SA 3.0. No endorsement
  by the photographer is implied.
- **Bundled file:** `references/memorial_church/front_king_of_hearts.jpg`.

The source image is packed inside the Main Quad `.blend`, so no separate texture
download is needed to open the released model.

## Architectural references

These sources informed geometry, proportions, and materials. Their photographs
are **not bundled or used as textures**, except for the licensed photograph above.

- Hoover Tower: https://architectplanning.stanford.edu/hoover-tower and
  https://www.hoover.org/events/hoover-tower-80th-anniversary
- Gates Computer Science Building: https://www.ramsa.com/projects/project/gates-computer-science-building
- Huang Engineering Center: https://engineering.stanford.edu/about/visit/location
  and https://www.office-52.com/work/jen-hsun-huang-engineering-center/
- Main Quad: https://visit.stanford.edu/explore-campus and
  https://quake06.stanford.edu/5-evolution-engineering-methods
- Memorial Church: https://orsl.stanford.edu/memorial-church-history
- Quad plan and planting: https://trees.stanford.edu/treewalks/treemaps.htm

More detailed reference ledgers are kept as text files under `references/` and
inside the Blender scenes. Research-only downloads, local session logs and
private working files are not included in this repository.
